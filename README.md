# Sit-TF-Down
A simple sitting mod for Boxroom. This is only compatible with the chairs from official catalogues. Does not work with workshop imported models.

**NOTE: Do NOT sit in a chair below a ceiling object (such as a light), you will be stuck and forced to sit in another chair.**

**NOTE: This mod IS compatible with my other mod, [Boxroom-TV](https://github.com/scumgr33n/Boxroom-TV). You shouldn't have any problems running both at the same time.**

<h1>HOW TO INSTALL</h1>

Install [MelonLoader 7.3](https://github.com/lavagang/melonloader) for Boxroom.

**Launch the game once to build your Mods folder.**

Drop SitTFDown.dll into your generated Mods folder and re-launch. 

<h1>HOW TO USE</h1>

Simply look at one of the chairs **(must be from an in-game catalogue)** and right click.

Left shift to stand again.

You can press Z to zoom the camera. 

<h1>KNOWN ISSUES</h1>

If you attempt to stand while sitting in a chair under a ceiling decoration (e.g. hanging light) then there is a high chance of the player becoming stuck.

To get unstuck, either sit in another chair and stand or spam crouch and jump until you're free.
